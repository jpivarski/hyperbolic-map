The vast dungeon

Rooms of a video-game dungeon where every room has five doors, so the floor plan cannot be laid flat. Drag to walk around, drag the outer ring to turn, and use the mouse wheel to zoom out until the whole infinite world fits in the disk — where a world-turtle is carrying it.

Our hero is in room 0-0. The others are numbered by row and column; rows curve, because that is the only way to fit in all the extra rooms.

…

The turtle and the star field are not library features. They are two image layers defined in demo/layers.js: one rotates with the disk, one does not. The 2012 version had shellImage and backgroundImage options baked into the viewer; this shows the same look built from outside it.

The rooms, doors and numbers are generated on demand, one cell of the tiling at a time — only the video-game characters come from dungeon.json. Far from the origin this single-patch version starts to break down; compare the tiled version.
