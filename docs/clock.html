A hyperbolic clock

A clock face with room for every second of twelve hours — 43,200 second ticks, 720 minute ticks and 12 hour numerals — because in the hyperbolic plane there is always more room further out. Scroll outward along the hands to read finer and finer divisions.

…

The hands are a second data source with its own rotation, updated once a second via setSourceTransform. That is an O(1) matrix change: the hand geometry is never rebuilt. The 2012 version rebuilt every hand drawable on every tick, and re-registered its setInterval each time, so after n minutes there were 60n timers running.
