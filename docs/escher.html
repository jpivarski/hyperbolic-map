Escher’s Circle Limit III

A hand-traced copy of Escher’s woodcut, drawn as 38,640 hyperbolic polygons. Drag inside the disk to scroll; drag the outer ring to rotate. Zoom is disabled here, matching the 2012 original.

The fish only approximately tile: the 2012 replication used an admittedly approximate group, and the art peters out a few layers from the centre. The tiled version puts one exact tile of fish on a true {8,3} tiling instead, and repeats it forever.
