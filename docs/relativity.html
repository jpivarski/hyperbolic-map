Compass mode

The same viewer with rotationMode: "compass". Scroll around and notice that “up” stays up: the view is continuously counter-rotated so that one fixed direction keeps a constant bearing on screen.

…

The default, parallel-transport, applies no correction: the view rotates by whatever holonomy the path accumulates. That is the geometrically natural behaviour, and going round a loop leaves you facing a different way — a direct consequence of curvature. Switch between the two above and drag in a circle to feel the difference.

Compass mode necessarily gives something up. Holding a bearing fixed means rotating about the centre of the screen, which moves the point under your cursor; you cannot both pin the grabbed point and pin the compass. The 2012 viewer made the same trade.
